# Saattvikaabhinaya in relation to Angika and Vaachika abhinaya : A critical textual and performative analysis of kuchipudi with reference to textual traditions

**Keywords** :
Satwam, Abhinaya, Satvika abhinaya, Angika abhinaya, Vachika abhinaya, Bhava, Vibhava, Anubhava, Rasa theory, Nayaka, Nayika, padam, javali, kuchipudi nritya natikas

**Introduction and background** :

    Yatho Hasta Thatho dristi, atho Dristi Thato Manah
    Yatho mana tatho Bhava, Yatho bhava thatho Rasa

My topic is all about saatvikabhinayam relating to angika and vachika. Dance is a beautiful art that acts as an essential source of communication. At the end ever performance of an art communicates something to the world. When a perfromance is going on, the context of topic can be clear and felt by the audience when the emotion perfectly delivered by the artist. When we talk about dance it can only be done with perfect & suitable abhinaya whether it might be angika, vachika, satwika.Though we present with proper angika and vachika, if the artist cannot present it with clear and appropriate "saatwikabhinaya" that can't produce rasa within audience. That might not attain success. It definitely can be done with the help of angika and vachika where angika involves meaningful gestures and limbs to the meaning of the lirics or vachika.

**The concept of Saattvikaabhinaya :**
The Saattvikaabhinaya it self contains the important word called "Sattva" The abhinaya through sattva means where the emotion built, arise and felt within the artist and made audience to feel the same. The poet, performer and auidence will align and be in same state of emotion. The atist should immerse the environment him and expresses emotions as another person which he is exactly not.

**Research gap and problem statement :**

Every classical dancer dances to the song with help of music but some only can experience the bhava and generate rasa. Even though they express the emotion they might not even know what bhava or rasa they performed. Some may not even create the magic of performance, they perform by just giving some expressions. In regard of this many of the younger learners have a doubt on what particlar expression should give at a particular scenario. So this documentation will definetely helps the young artists in both theoritical and practical way that how one should take the lirics, get the meaning of it, how bhava, vibhava, anubhava helps to generate expression and then Rasa in rasikas. In a performative way

    "Bhaava, vibhaava, anubhaava samyogaath Rasa Nishpatti"

**Methodology**:

1.  Deep study of sattva, abhinaya, saatvika, angika, vachika,abhinayas
2.  Analising the theorical aspects involved relavent to abhinaya such as bhava, vibhava, anubhava, nayaka, nayika, rasa, padam, javali, nrutya natika
3.  Involvement of angikabhinaya which helps to deliver the exact meaning
4.  Mapping vaachikabhinaya the lirics through which artist can involve and experience the emotion
5.  For practical analysation: Collection of data (song, lirics, story, music) such as a kuchipudi nrutya natika, padam and javali as examples for clear explanation of each individual topic
6.  Take example of any particular nrutya natika (eg: bhamakalapam, where there is more importance of vachika, angika and saatwikabhinayam) and pull out the rasas involved within the characters, the bhavas those travelled in between characters of bhamakalapam howthey immerse and execute the expression to generate rasa in audience
7.  Take the example of padam/javali
    - Understand the word by word meaning of song by each lirics from beginning to ending
    - finding out the main rasa that hero and heroine depicts and floats in the upper layer
8.  Interview the kuchipudi dance gurus, choreographers, artists that how they perform and their experience of aubhava on stage, and the theories they follow to make it best
